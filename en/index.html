How the immersion works

Day 1 — See. You see the pyramid, diagnose where you are, and choose your real intention.

Day 2 — Structure. You fill in the 5 pillars on the Canvas for your use case.

Day 3 — Build. You turn the Canvas into a system that runs and improves itself.

Day 4 — Shift. You migrate the system to the Sep/2026 generation (Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra): fewer steps, more intention, boundaries, and a definition of done.

Each day has practical exercises, ready-made examples, and a reusable template.
